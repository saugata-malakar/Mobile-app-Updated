1. Overview & Scope

DiabetesCare AI is a clinical screening and community triage platform designed for authorized Doctors, ASHA/ICDS/CHO field health workers, Clinical Moderators, and Hospital Administrators to document, quality-check, and triage diabetic foot screening cases. This Privacy Policy explains how personal and sensitive clinical data are collected, processed, stored, protected, and deleted in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and Google Play Health & User Data Policies.

Medical Disclaimer: DiabetesCare AI is a clinical documentation, image-quality screening, and tele-triage decision-support tool. It does not replace independent clinical judgment or formal medical diagnosis by a licensed physician.

2. Data We Collect & Why

Category Specific Data Collected Purpose of Processing
Health & Clinical Records Patient name/identifier, age, weight (kg), blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), blood sugar (mg/dL), comorbidities, optional previous medical reports, and doctor clinical notes (diagnosis, severity, treatment, medication). Clinical screening, longitudinal follow-up (e.g., 1-month review), moderator quality verification, and physician triage.
Clinical Wound / Foot Photos Standardized 3-photo screening sequence (distance, closer, even closer with calibration coin) captured via camera or uploaded reports. Automated image-quality verification (focus, framing, lighting, calibration reference) and clinician visual review.
Healthcare Worker & User Account Data Full name, email address, mobile phone number, username / worker ID, role (Doctor, ASHA/ICDS/CHO Field Worker, Moderator, Admin), worker type, district, and optional specialization. Role-based authentication (RBAC), clinical audit trail, submission attribution, and verified field-worker incentive payouts.
Audit & Diagnostic Telemetry API access timestamps, consent acknowledgment status, and moderation decision logs. DPDP Act 2023 compliance auditing, security monitoring, and fraud prevention.

Zero Advertising or Data Brokerage: DiabetesCare AI contains no third-party advertising SDKs, does not track users across third-party apps or websites, and never sells or rents personal or clinical health data.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly on-demand when a Doctor or ASHA/ICDS/CHO worker captures the guided 3-photo clinical screening sequence. No background or covert capture occurs.
  • Photos / Media Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android ≤ 12): Used only when a clinician attaches optional previous medical reports or selects a clinical image for upload.
  • Network / Internet (INTERNET): Used to transmit encrypted clinical submissions, authentication tokens, and triage updates to the authorized backend server.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used solely for subtle tactile feedback during guided capture steps.

4. Legal Basis & Explicit Consent (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, personal and sensitive clinical data are collected only after obtaining clear, itemized consent from the Data Principal (patient or authorized healthcare worker):

  • Field workers and doctors confirm patient verbal/written consent prior to capturing clinical images and vitals.
  • Users may withdraw consent or request correction/erasure of personal data at any time (see Section 7).

5. Data Sharing & Access Controls

Clinical records are accessible strictly on a need-to-know, role-based basis:

  • Field Workers (ASHA/ICDS/CHO): Can view only their own submitted screening records and review statuses.
  • Moderators: Review submitted image quality and patient vitals to approve or return cases for re-capture.
  • Doctors: Review moderator-approved cases and manage clinical notes and follow-ups.
  • Hospital Administrators: Audit platform activity, manage user verification, and reconcile approved clinical payouts.

Data are not shared with external commercial parties. De-identified or aggregated clinical research outputs are used solely under institutional ethics approval by IIT Kharagpur and MMCH.

6. Security Safeguards & Retention

  • Encryption in Transit: All production communications between the mobile app, web dashboard, and backend API use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Authentication & Access Security: Passwords are stored using cryptographic hashing; sessions use signed JWT tokens with role-based endpoint enforcement.
  • Retention: Clinical records are retained for active patient follow-up care and statutory medical record retention requirements, or until an authorized erasure request is processed.

7. Your Rights, Account Deletion & Data Erasure

In compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 and Google Play's Data Deletion Policy, both healthcare workers and patients have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of their account and associated personal data:

  • In-App / Admin Request: Contact your Hospital Administrator via the Admin Portal to deactivate your account or initiate DPDP erasure.
  • Email Deletion Request: Send a request with your registered User ID, Email, or Phone Number (and Patient ID, if applicable) to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@diabetescare-ai.iitkgp.ac.in.
  • Resolution Timeline: Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 calendar days, except where retention is mandatorily required by applicable Indian clinical/legal regulations (in which case personal identifiers are anonymized).

8. Children's Privacy

DiabetesCare AI is intended exclusively for use by adult healthcare professionals, community health workers, and clinical administrators (18+ years). It is not directed toward children.
